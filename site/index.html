How it works

Three steps. Your team stays in charge.

Every agent follows the same path, so your staff learns it once.

1

Enter the facts

Add an event, a donor, a grant, a volunteer, or meeting notes. Agents only work from what you enter.

2

Review the drafts

Emails, posts, reports, and minutes arrive as drafts. Anything missing is marked for you to fill in.

3

Approve and finish

An approver signs off, then a typed confirmation completes the step. Every action is recorded.

Built for trust

A person stays in charge, and the software enforces it.

We don't rely on the AI to behave. The rules are built into the app itself.

  • Only facts you enter. Drafts never invent dates, prices, names, or links. Gaps become placeholders.
  • No approval with gaps. A draft with a placeholder can't be approved or sent.
  • Consent first. No agent-drafted email goes to a donor, member, or volunteer who hasn't agreed to hear from you.
  • Receipts and minutes are protected. Receipt numbers are set in order, and an issued receipt can't be issued again. Minutes can't be marked approved while a motion needs confirming.
  • A full record. Every draft, approval, and send is logged, and each organization's data is kept separate.
